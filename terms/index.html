What Plateful is

Plateful is a calorie and macronutrient tracker. It estimates what is in a meal from a description, a photo, a barcode or a nutrition label, and keeps a log of what you confirm.

Every number Plateful shows is an estimate for informational purposes. It is not medical, nutritional or dietary advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, dietitian or other qualified professional. Do not rely on it to manage a medical condition, an allergy or an eating disorder.

The meal score is a guide computed from a meal's nutrients, its size and where its numbers came from. It is not a health rating and not medical advice.

Health and safety

Talk to a doctor before you start a diet or change how you eat, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, have a medical condition, or have or have had an eating disorder. Suggested daily targets, adaptive targets and energy-balance figures come from general formulas and what you enter, and may not suit you.

Estimates can be wrong about what a meal contains, including allergens. Never rely on Plateful to decide whether a food is safe for you to eat. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your doctor or emergency services immediately.

Estimates and food data

Plateful estimates meals with models on your iPhone, its built-in food table and, when you allow it, models in the cloud. Portions, recipes and ingredients vary, and automated models make mistakes. Check an estimate before you confirm it, and correct it when it is wrong. We do not promise that any estimate, food record or target is accurate, complete or right for you.

Nutrition figures come from USDA FoodData Central (public domain) and Open Food Facts (Open Database License, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Open Food Facts is community-maintained, so a scanned product's numbers come from whoever entered them. Check the packet if something looks wrong.
